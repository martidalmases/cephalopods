    Month by month

     

    For each ticked variable we take how much the place has changed over the whole period (Sen's slope × number of years) and divide it by how much it swings from one year to the next. A value of 2 means the place has moved two typical swings away from where it started. Variables whose trend is not significant count as 0. The index is the mean over the ticked variables: it shows where the sea is changing most, whatever the variable.
